# Repurly Policy and Legal Readiness Review

Prepared: 14 May 2026

## Status
Repurly is policy-hardened for internal use and controlled founder pilots. It is not yet solicitor-reviewed for broad public SaaS launch.

## Strengths
- Human-in-the-loop positioning.
- No scraping / no automated DMs / no fake engagement wording.
- Campaign/output channel wording avoids overclaiming direct API publishing.
- Subscription plan limits are documented.
- Draft Terms, Privacy, Acceptable Use Policy, DPA template and Subprocessor Register are included.

## Must complete before broad launch
- Solicitor review of Terms, Privacy, DPA and AUP.
- Confirm production subprocessors, regions and contracts.
- Confirm cancellation/refund support flow.
- Test Stripe checkout, portal and webhooks.
- Test plan-limit enforcement.
- Confirm all platform integration claims are accurate.
- Finalise support address and privacy contact.
