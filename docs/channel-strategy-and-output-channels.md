# Channel Strategy and Output Channels

Repurly should be sold as a LinkedIn-led Growth OS with supporting campaign/output channels.

## Why this matters

The phrase **connected channels** can imply that every listed platform has a live, tested direct-publishing integration. That creates commercial and support risk.

Use **campaign/output channels** when describing plan limits and cross-channel value. A campaign/output channel is a destination Repurly helps plan, adapt, approve, export, or publish to where an integration is enabled.

## Launch-safe channel list

- LinkedIn post
- LinkedIn carousel outline
- Facebook Page post
- Instagram caption
- Google Business Profile update
- Email/newsletter
- Blog post
- YouTube title, description, chapters, and script
- TikTok/Reels hook, caption, and shot list
- Threads post
- X post/thread export
- WhatsApp/referral message

## Direct-publish rule

Only describe a channel as directly connected/published when the provider app, permissions, webhook flow, and end-to-end publishing test are complete.

## Safety rule

Repurly must not promise scraping, automated DMs, fake engagement, auto-commenting, auto-liking, or account-risk automation.

## Recommended public wording

> Repurly is LinkedIn-led at launch, with export and campaign workflows for supporting channels.

> Turn one idea into approved, campaign-ready assets for LinkedIn and supporting channels.
