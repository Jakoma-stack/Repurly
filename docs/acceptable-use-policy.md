# Repurly Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026

Repurly is a human-in-the-loop campaign and growth workflow system. Users must not use it for:

- scraping or unauthorised collection of personal data;
- automated LinkedIn direct messages, automated connection requests, auto-comments, auto-likes or fake engagement;
- spam, harassment, intimidation, discrimination or unlawful marketing;
- misleading, fraudulent or unsubstantiated claims;
- impersonation or deceptive identity practices;
- processing sensitive personal data without authority and a lawful basis;
- uploading data that the user is not authorised to use;
- malware, security abuse or attempts to bypass platform controls;
- activity that breaches LinkedIn, Meta, X, TikTok, YouTube, Google or other third-party platform terms.

Customers remain responsible for final content approval, publication decisions, outreach conduct and compliance with applicable marketing, privacy and platform rules.
