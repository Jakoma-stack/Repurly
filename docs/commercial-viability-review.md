# Commercial Viability Review

## Positioning

Repurly is strongest when positioned as a LinkedIn-led revenue workflow system, not a generic social scheduler. The product should focus on campaign planning, brand/offer context, draft creation, approval routing, CTA tracking, lead notes, and human-approved follow-up.

## Market reality

Low-cost tools like Buffer and Later compete heavily on scheduling price. Enterprise tools such as Hootsuite and Sprout Social compete on broad channel management, analytics, inbox, and team reporting. Repurly should avoid competing directly as a broad scheduler.

The monetisable wedge is narrower and more valuable:

- consultants and agencies who need repeatable LinkedIn-led campaigns
- B2B operators managing multiple offers
- fractional marketers who need approval workflows and lead notes
- founder-led firms that want content connected to booked calls and pipeline

## Pricing logic

- Starter at £79/mo is higher than basic schedulers because it is positioned around campaign workflow, not channel posting.
- Operator at £249/mo is the main plan because it unlocks multi-offer use, approvals, lead notes, reports, and team usage.
- Studio from £699/mo should remain application-led and/or assisted because it creates support and onboarding expectations.
- Founder Pilot at £950 is the most realistic first monetisation route before self-serve scale.

## Launch recommendation

Launch in this order:

1. Internal Jakoma use across B2B consulting, Independent Living Tech, toolkits, and Repurly itself.
2. 3-5 paid Founder Pilots at £950.
3. Convert successful pilots to Operator at £249/mo.
4. Offer Studio or done-with-you content operations only when onboarding and reporting are stable.

## Risk controls

LinkedIn-led does not mean LinkedIn automation. Repurly should remain human-in-the-loop and avoid scraping, automated DMs, fake engagement, browser automation, or account-risk activity.


## Channel strategy

The safest commercial wording is **campaign/output channels**, not broad **connected channels**. Repurly should support multi-channel campaign production without implying every destination has a live, tested direct-publish API.

Launch order:

1. LinkedIn as the primary workflow.
2. Facebook Page and Instagram Business as assisted export or direct publishing only where the Meta setup is configured and tested.
3. Google Business Profile, email, blog, and WhatsApp as practical export workflows.
4. YouTube and TikTok as scripts, descriptions, hooks, captions, shot lists, and upload checklists first.
5. Threads and X as manual export channels unless paying customers create a clear integration need.

Public promise:

> Repurly is LinkedIn-led at launch, with export and campaign workflows for supporting channels.

Avoid promising automatic posting everywhere.
