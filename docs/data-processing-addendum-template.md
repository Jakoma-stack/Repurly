# Repurly Data Processing Addendum - Working Template

Last updated: 14 May 2026

This is a working template for solicitor review before broad public SaaS launch.

## Roles
Repurly may act as processor where it processes customer personal data inside workspaces, campaign content, lead notes or approval workflows on behalf of a business customer.

The customer remains controller for its customer, lead, staff, prospect and campaign data.

## Processing scope
- Account and workspace administration
- Campaign planning and content workflows
- Approval notes and lead notes
- Billing state and subscription management
- Integration metadata and logs
- Support and troubleshooting

## Customer obligations
The customer must ensure it has a lawful basis and authority to upload or process personal data in Repurly.

## Repurly obligations
Repurly should:
- process customer personal data only to provide and secure the service;
- apply appropriate technical and organisational measures;
- limit access to authorised personnel and providers;
- support reasonable deletion/export requests;
- notify customers of relevant security incidents without undue delay;
- maintain a subprocessor register before broad public launch.

## Subprocessors
See `docs/subprocessor-register.md`.

## International transfers
Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, suitable safeguards should be used where required.

## Deletion and return
On termination, customer data should be exportable or deleted in accordance with the service's retention and backup practices.
