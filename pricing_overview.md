# Repurly pricing overview

## Positioning
Repurly is a premium LinkedIn-first workflow product for agencies and B2B teams that need drafting, approvals, scheduling, queue visibility, recovery, engagement workflow, and multi-brand control in one platform.

## Plans

### Core — £297/mo
- Up to 2 workspace members
- 1 brand
- 120 posts per month
- 3 connected channels
- LinkedIn composer, queue, job detail, and recovery

### Growth — £697/mo
- Up to 5 workspace members
- Up to 3 brands
- 1,000 posts per month
- 10 connected channels
- Approval flows
- AI-assisted drafting
- Engagement workflow, reports, and notifications

### Scale — Custom
- Custom workspace and brand limits
- Higher publishing volume
- 30+ connected-channel capacity by agreement
- Priority support
- Commercial onboarding support

### Founder pilot — £950 one-off

Use this only for the first proof customers where the founder is directly involved and the goal is evidence, testimonials, workflow feedback and case material. Cap this offer tightly so it does not become the long-term price.

Includes:
- Workspace setup
- Offer and brand mapping
- First campaign structure
- First 30 days of LinkedIn content workflow
- Review session and conversion notes

### Guided pilot / onboarding — from £1,500 one-off
- Guided setup
- Workflow and channel configuration
- User onboarding
- Governance and workflow tuning

## Why this pricing exists
Repurly is priced:
- above lightweight scheduling tools
- below heavyweight enterprise social suites
- around the real value of premium LinkedIn workflow control

## Current sales rule
Sell Core and Growth as self-serve or assisted checkout plans. Use the £950 founder pilot only as a limited early proof offer, then move guided onboarding to £1,500+. Treat Scale as a manual sales conversation until the higher-volume onboarding path is proven with live customers.
